Our Story

Why Karacel?

Founded in 2017 by members of Sukhoi Squadron, Entebbe Air Force Wing, Karacel Association was born out of a simple realisation — monthly earners face financial challenges that can only be solved by pooling resources with discipline.

Formerly known as Sukhoi Association, we were renamed Karacel Association during our 2nd AGM on 17th January 2025 — a name that reflects our spirit of togetherness and shared prosperity.
