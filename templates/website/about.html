About Karacel Association

Together We Save. Together We Grow. Always There For You.

Who We Are

A community built on trust, unity, and shared prosperity

Karacel Association is a member-owned savings and credit association founded in 2017 by the personnel of Sukhoi Squadron, Entebbe Air Force Wing. What began as a self-help initiative among monthly earners has grown into a disciplined savings and credit cooperative serving our community.

Originally named Sukhoi Association, we were renamed Karacel Association (KA) during our 2nd AGM on 17th January 2025. Our financial year runs from January to December, with savings contributions that have no period.

Community first

Our Mission

To confront financial constraints of the squadron personnel through a self-help group initiative.

Our Vision

To grow into a self-sustained financial reservoir that uses its own generated funds to mitigate financial constraints within its members.

Our Motto

"Always There For You."

What Drives Us

Our Objectives

  • 01

  • 02

  • 03

  • 04

Governance

Guided by Two Committees

Karacel is governed by a Management Committee (Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, and Publicity) and a Supervisory Committee drawn from squadron command. The three highest shareholders of a closing financial year form the succeeding year's Committee of Trustees.
