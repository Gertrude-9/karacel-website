About Karacel Association

Together We Save. Together We Grow. Together We Support.

Who We Are

A community built on trust, unity, and shared prosperity

Karacel Association is a member-owned financial cooperative (SACCO) established to promote a culture of saving, provide accessible credit, and offer compassionate welfare support to our members and their families. We believe in the power of community and shared prosperity.

Our name "Karacel" reflects our commitment to togetherness — because we achieve more when we stand united.

Community first

Our Mission

To empower our members with sustainable financial services, promote a disciplined savings culture, and provide a strong social safety net through mutual support.

Our Vision

To be the leading member-driven financial association, recognized for integrity, innovation, and unwavering commitment to the wellbeing of our community.

What Drives Us

Our Objectives

  • 01

    Encourage regular savings among members to build financial security.

  • 02

    Provide affordable and timely credit facilities to meet members' needs.

  • 03

    Offer condolence and welfare support during bereavement and difficult times.

  • 04

    Promote financial literacy and responsible money management.

  • 05

    Foster a spirit of unity, trust, and mutual assistance among members.
