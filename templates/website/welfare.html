Step by step

How the Fund Works

From registration to payout — here's the full journey of your condolence cover.

01

Register & Pay Your Annual Contribution

Pay UGX 100,000 per year to activate your condolence cover. This amount is held in the association's condolence fund for the entire year.

02

Register Up to 10 Family Members

Submit the names of up to 10 family members you wish to be covered. These are the beneficiaries whose passing would make you eligible for support from the fund.

03

When a Loss Occurs

If any registered member loses one of their listed family members, the association is notified. Every member then contributes UGX 20,000 to support the bereaved family.

04

Top-Up Rule — Keeping the Fund Intact

If the year ends with no loss, your UGX 100,000 remains intact for the next year. If a loss occurs and the fund is used, each member simply tops up their account back to UGX 100,000 — ensuring the fund is always ready for the next need.

The Top-Up Rule Explained

Unlike traditional insurance, your contribution is not "lost" if unused. Here's how the fund stays healthy year after year.

Year with no loss
UGX 100,000 stays with you for next year
Year with a loss
UGX 20,000 contributed per member
After the loss
Top-Up back to UGX 100,000

A Real-World Example

Let's walk through a full year to see exactly how the KAC fund moves.

1

January: Amina pays her UGX 100,000 annual contribution and registers 10 family members — her parents, siblings, and children.

2

June: Sadly, one of Amina's registered family members passes away. The association is notified, and the bereaved family receives condolence support.

3

July: Every member contributes UGX 20,000. This pooled amount is delivered to the bereaved family as condolence.

4

After payout: Because the fund was used, each member simply tops up UGX 100,000 — restoring the fund to full strength for any future losses.

5

December: The year ends. No further losses occurred, so Amina's UGX 100,000 remains intact and rolls over into the next year automatically.
