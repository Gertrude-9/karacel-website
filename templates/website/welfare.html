Karacel Condolences & Welfare

A compassionate fund that ensures you and your loved ones receive meaningful support during life's most difficult moments — because no member should grieve alone.

How KAC Works at a Glance

Every member pays UGX 100,000 each year — with or without a loss. You may register up to 10 family members as beneficiaries. When a registered family member passes away, each member contributes UGX 20,000. When the account owner (the member) passes away, each member contributes UGX 40,000.

Plan overview

The Numbers That Matter

Everything you need to know about the Karacel Condolences (KAC) plan, at a glance.

Annual Contribution

UGX 100,000 every year — mandatory

Your yearly contribution keeps you and your registered family members covered for condolence and death support throughout the year. This is paid regardless of whether a claim is made.

Registered Family Members

Up to 10 beneficiaries of your choice

Choose up to 10 family members whose passing would make you eligible for condolence support from the association's fund.

Contribution — Registered Loss

UGX 20,000 from each member

When a registered family member of any member passes away, every member contributes UGX 20,000 — pooled together to give the bereaved family meaningful condolence support.

Contribution — Member Passing

UGX 40,000 from each member

When the account owner (the member) themselves passes away, every member contributes UGX 40,000 — a larger contribution that reflects the gravity of losing a member of the SACCO family.

Step by step

How the Fund Works

From registration to payout — here's the full journey of your cover.

01

Pay Your Annual Contribution

Pay UGX 100,000 every year. This is required whether or not a loss occurs — the pool must always remain healthy to support any member in their time of grief.

02

Register Up to 10 Family Members

Submit the names of up to 10 family members you wish to be covered. These are the beneficiaries whose passing would make you eligible for condolence support from the fund.

03

When a Registered Member Passes Away

If a member loses one of their listed family members, the association is notified. Every member then contributes UGX 20,000 to support the bereaved family with condolence.

04

When the Account Owner Passes Away

If a member themselves passes away, every other member contributes UGX 40,000 — a larger amount, because the loss is felt by the entire SACCO family and the member's household loses its anchor.

05

Annual Renewal — Every Year

The UGX 100,000 annual contribution is paid every year by every member. This is a fixed yearly obligation — the fund is not a one-off payment, and it does not depend on whether a claim was made.

Two contribution tiers

Who Passed Away Determines the Contribution

Every loss touches the SACCO family, but the amount each member contributes depends on who the loss is.

Tier 1 — Condolence
A registered family member passes away
UGX 20,000 contributed by every member

The affected member's registered beneficiary has passed away. Every other member contributes UGX 20,000 to give the bereaved family condolence support. The selected register slot is permanently closed and never replaced.

Tier 2 — Death of a Member
The account owner passes away
UGX 40,000 contributed by every member

A SACCO member has passed away. Every other member contributes UGX 40,000 to support the member's household. The contribution is larger because the loss strikes at the core of the SACCO family.

A Real-World Example

Let's walk through a full year to see exactly how the KAC fund moves.

1

January: Amina pays her UGX 100,000 annual contribution and registers 10 family members — her parents, siblings, and children.

2

June: Sadly, one of Amina's registered family members passes away. The association is notified. Every member contributes UGX 20,000 — the condolence tier.

3

October: In the same year, a member of the SACCO passes away. Every member contributes UGX 40,000 — the death tier. The member's household receives the pooled condolence.

4

December: The year ends. Amina's UGX 100,000 has been paid for the year — this was mandatory, regardless of any claims. Next year, she pays the same amount again.

✓

Summary for the year: Amina paid UGX 100,000 annual contribution, and — through the pool — contributed UGX 20,000 for the registered loss and UGX 40,000 for the member's passing. Her registered cover remains active for the rest of the year.
