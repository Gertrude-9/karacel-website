Loans Built Around Your Savings

At Karacel Association, your savings unlock your borrowing power. The more you save, the more you can access — with fair interest rates and flexible guarantor terms.

Guarantor Rule at a Glance

Loans up to 95% of your savings require no guarantor. Loans above 95% of your savings require two guarantors who act as collateral — if you fail to repay, they refund the loan on your behalf.

Guarantor requirements

When Do You Need a Guarantor?

Your guarantor requirement depends purely on how much of your savings you're borrowing. Here's the simple rule.

No Guarantor Needed

Easy access

If you are borrowing 95% of your savings or less, you can access your loan immediately — no guarantors required.

  • Fast approval process
  • No paperwork from third parties
  • Your own savings act as security
  • Perfect for emergency needs

Two Guarantors Required

Additional security

If you are borrowing above 95% of your savings, you must provide two guarantors. They act as collateral for the loan.

  • Both guarantors must be registered members
  • They pledge to cover the loan if you default
  • Their savings act as additional security
  • Ensures the association stays protected
