Fully Automatic

The system computes KS interest automatically at the end of every month, using the interest paid on loan repayments received during that month.

  • 10% → Contingency fund
  • 90% → Distributed to all KS savers
  • Per-member share: (member KS × distributable) ÷ total KS pool
